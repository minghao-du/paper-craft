# Tmux helper

Use with the common lifecycle in [pc-helper](../SKILL.md).

## Start

Launch the selected runner with its documented input options, record its returned pane as `ID` and any actual native session identifier as `SID`, and verify the directory and startup.
A native session id may be absent in this branch; keep the pane identity distinct from agent liveness.
If launch is unavailable, leave the brief prepared and do not record a running helper.

## Confirm reports

Verify the first result in `result.md` and record any pending correction there; native messages additionally require a supported authorized channel.

## Talk and resume

Read `result.md` first. Use the selected runner's supported resume behavior for a new round.
When a supported authorized messaging channel exists, send to its exact returned identity; otherwise record pending instructions in its home.

## Stop

Stop only the recorded owned runner/pane through its supported mechanism and verify termination; keep evidence and checkouts until the common preservation gates pass.

## Sweep

Report pane presence separately from runner liveness and label unavailable evidence unknown.
